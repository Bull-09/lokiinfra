Our story

From building Surat to managing India’s builds.

In 1993, Mr. Sanjay Shinkar founded {{PARENT}} in Surat. Over three decades the company delivered more than 500 projects, including city roads, paver and drainage networks, structural repairs, waterproofing, commercial renovations and solar installations. It earned its reputation one site at a time.

Over those years we saw the same story on project after project: owners with large budgets and no one on site truly representing them. Contractors manage contractors, costs drift, timelines slip, and quality becomes a negotiation.

LOKI Infra was founded to change that. We are a dedicated project management consultancy that brings the group’s hands-on construction experience to the owner’s side of the table, for high-value industrial and commercial projects across India.
