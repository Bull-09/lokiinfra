The short answer

What is a project management consultancy (PMC)?

A project management consultancy (PMC) is an independent firm that manages a construction project on the owner’s behalf. It plans the schedule and budget, runs tenders, coordinates every contractor, certifies bills, enforces quality and safety, and hands the finished building over. A PMC does not build. It makes sure the builders deliver what the owner is paying for.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about PMC services

What does a PMC do on a construction project?

A PMC plans the programme and budget, prepares tender packages, evaluates bids, coordinates all contractors on site, measures and certifies bills, manages variations, runs quality and safety checks, and manages testing, commissioning and handover. It acts as the owner’s single representative throughout the project.

How are PMC fees charged in India?

PMC fees are usually charged as a percentage of the project’s construction cost or as a fixed monthly fee for the project duration. The figure depends on scope, project size and how long the PMC is engaged. LOKI Infra quotes after a site visit and agrees the fee in writing before work starts.

When should an owner appoint a PMC?

Ideally at the feasibility or early design stage. That is when value engineering, budget setting and tender strategy save the most money. A PMC can also take over a project already under construction, typically to recover a delayed schedule or bring costs under control.

Is a PMC the same as an architect or a contractor?

No. The architect designs the building and the contractor builds it. A PMC sits between them on the owner’s side, making sure the design is buildable within budget and that the contractor delivers it on time, to specification and safely.
