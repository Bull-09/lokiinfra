Questions

What owners usually ask first.

What does a PMC actually do on my project?

We act as your representative on site. We plan the work, run the tenders, manage every contractor, certify bills, enforce quality and safety, and hand the plant over complete.

How are your fees structured?

Usually as a percentage of project cost or a fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing before work starts. We quote after a site visit and a look at your scope.

When should we bring a PMC in?

As early as you can, ideally before the design is final. Value engineering and tender strategy save the most money at that stage. We also take over projects already underway.

Do you work outside Gujarat?

Yes. We are based in Surat and focus on Gujarat, and we manage projects anywhere in India.

Can LOKI Infra build the project as well?

Yes. If you prefer one contract and one point of responsibility, we offer design and build delivery through our group’s own execution teams.
