# Hairline figures for the LOKI Infra site

Generate these with `/hairline-create` in a new session, save each result here using the
file name shown (`.html` output is fine; the build pulls the `<svg>` out of it), then run
`python3 build.py`. Any figure you haven't made yet keeps its current placeholder art.

**Style notes for every figure:** thin line work on a dark ink background (#0F1720), lines in
pale steel blue (#8DB4D3) with one or two amber (#F2A900) accents. No fills or text.

| File | Replaces | Prompt for /hairline-create |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.html` | Homepage hero drawing | An industrial campus under construction: sawtooth-roof factory shed, a G+3 admin block, and a tower crane lifting a load (crane in amber) |
| `factory.html` | Industrial sector cards | A manufacturing plant with a sawtooth roof and a chimney |
| `warehouse.html` | Warehousing cards | A large PEB warehouse with a row of loading docks |
| `office.html` | Commercial office cards | A glass-grid corporate office tower with low side wings |
| `retail.html` | Retail & hospitality cards | A mall or showroom block with a front canopy |
| `hospital.html` | Healthcare & education cards | A hospital building with a cross on the roof block |
| `residential.html` | Residential & township cards | Two residential high-rise towers of different heights |
| `infra.html` | Roads & civil works cards | A road receding to the horizon with a bridge over it |
| `solar.html` | Solar & renewables cards | Two rows of tilted solar panels under a sun |
